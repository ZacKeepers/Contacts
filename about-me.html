About Me

I am Zachary Keepers, a mechanical engineering student at the University of Alabama with a passion for robotics and automotive design.

Personal Narrative & Background

My entry into engineering started at home through my dad, an aerospace engineer who worked on extravehicular interfaces for astronauts on the International Space Station. Hearing his stories, I became fascinated by how complex mechanisms seamlessly come together to form larger, functional systems.

That curiosity found a purpose in 4th grade when I attended the Rocket City Regional robotics competition. Seeing giant machines zip across the field and launch game pieces, I knew immediately that I wanted to build and compete with robots like that. In 5th grade, I founded my first FIRST LEGO League (FLL) team, Team C.R.A.B., which went on to win state championships and compete on the global stage. Over the years, I transitioned to high school FIRST Tech Challenge (FTC), where I served as Systems Integrator, Captain, and Chief Engineer, and helped found a community FIRST Robotics Competition (FRC) team. In 2025, standing behind the driver station glass at the Arkansas Regional was the realization of a goal I set years prior. That same season, I was honored to be named a global FIRST Dean’s List Winner, one of 10 students selected worldwide.

Passions & What Drives Me

I thrive in the hands-on cycle of engineering: think, design, make, refine. For me, the excitement is not just seeing a finished robot, but navigating every step along the way. I love sketching by hand, modeling in CAD, operating CNC mills and waterjets, and integrating mechanical, electrical, and software components into a working system.

My technical interests focus on mechanical design, mechatronics, and automotive or experiential systems. Outside of engineering, I stay active by going to the gym and playing ultimate frisbee. I also apply my tech skills in diverse settings, such as serving as a soundboard technician for my church.

Core Values & Philosophy

My approach to engineering and leadership is guided by servant leadership and Gracious Professionalism. I believe true leadership is not about giving orders or seeking recognition. Instead, it is about offering a hand, empowering others, and building people while building projects.

Whether I am mentoring local and international robotics teams, training newer students in CAD and fabrication, or collaborating on group projects, my goal is to lift others as I climb. I aim to lead with patience, integrity, and humility so that every decision impacts my team and community positively.

Looking Ahead & Goals

I am pursuing my bachelor’s degree in mechanical engineering, with my sights set on entering the industry upon graduation. My career goals center on working in advanced robotics, experiential design, or automotive engineering, where I can apply my hands-on fabrication skills and technical creativity to build impactful systems.
