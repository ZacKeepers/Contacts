Cypher

Cypher was FIRST Tech Challenge Team 24331's 2025–2026 State and World Championship robot. As Chief Engineer, I led the strategy, prototyping, CAD design, and fabrication. Cypher was the Finalist Alliance Partner at the Alabama State Championship.

As Cypher is an old project its design history is not shown.

Overview

Cypher is FTC Team 24331 Caesar Circuitry's most consistent, intelligent, and elegant robot, built for the 2025–2026 season. It features a low-center-of-gravity custom aluminum chassis and a 270-degree rotating turret.

Intake / Transfer Assembly

The triple roller system operates at 1,650 RPM through miter gears and uses a servo-actuated stopper for precise artifact management. By combining fast Gecko wheels, transfer belts, and analog distance sensors, the robot reliably stores up to three artifacts while cutting down software loop time.

Parallel Plate Belt Drive & Drivetrain

The robot uses a custom aluminum parallel plate belt drive to maintain a low center of gravity and mecanum wheels for holonomic mobility. It is guided by Pedro Pathing and robot-oriented driving.

270° Rotating Turret

Driven by a custom 2:1 gear ratio and a REV Through Bore Encoder, the turret rotates 270 degrees within an 18-inch height limit. Controlled by a dual PID system, it continuously tracks targets automatically, saving an average of 20 seconds per match by eliminating manual goal alignment.

Fixed Hood Launcher

The launcher incorporates dual 6,000 RPM motors and a high-durometer weighted flywheel to provide fast spin-up times and consistent power across all three shots. Velocity profiles are optimized via Cubic Hermite Splines and managed with a custom PIDV controller to maintain steady transit velocities.

Control & Vision System (Limelight & Autonomous)

An Extended Kalman Filter (EKF) fuses GoBilda Pinpoint odometry with Limelight AprilTag vision to reduce localization drift from ±3 inches down to ±1 inch. Additionally, the system employs PathLegion for on-the-fly QR code path selection and Panels Dashboard for real-time PID tuning to achieve precise targeting.
